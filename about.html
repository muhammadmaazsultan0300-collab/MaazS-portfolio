About Me

A developer who likes solving real problems

I'm Muhammad Maaz Sultan, an AI Engineering student and developer based in Lahore, Pakistan, currently studying in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at UET Lahore. I enjoy turning real-world problems into practical software — the kind that's actually useful once it's shipped, not just impressive in a demo.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, web technologies, automation, machine learning, and AI engineering. I'm still early in that journey, and I'm intentional about it — building projects that stretch my skills across each of these areas rather than staying comfortable in just one.
